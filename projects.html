Auto Shop Invoicing CRM

Invoicing and customer CRM used every day by a real NYC auto repair shop.

NYC GPT — RAG Chatbot

A streaming AI chatbot that answers questions about New York City, grounded in retrieved Wikipedia sources.

  • RAG pipeline: scrape pages → chunk with LangChain.js → embed with OpenAI → AstraDB vector search → grounded GPT answer.
  • Answers stream token by token to the chat UI using the Vercel AI SDK.
  • Per-IP rate limiting plus message-length, history, and response-token caps to keep API cost bounded.

POS Invoice Manager

Point-of-sale and invoicing app built for Buffet Lucia’s Fiesta Mexicana, a Bronx catering business, and available to other small businesses.

  • Stripe card payments with the amount and credit-card surcharge computed on the server, not trusted from the browser.
  • Staff login, signed read-only invoice links, and printable invoices.
  • SMS specials broadcast through Twilio, sent only to customers who opted in.

No login needed: press “Try the demo” on the sign-in screen (sample data only).

Suly’s Sweets

Bilingual (English/Spanish) bakery website and holiday pre-order system for a Bronx home bakery.

  • Stripe Checkout for 50% deposits, with every price recomputed on the server from the product catalog.
  • Signature-verified Stripe webhook that sends email receipts through Resend.
  • Lighthouse-optimized, with Vitest tests (including an English/Spanish translation check) and CI.
